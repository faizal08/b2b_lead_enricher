How to find a phone number from a LinkedIn profile

All you need is the profile link.

  1. 1

    Copy the profile URL

    Open the person's LinkedIn profile and copy the link from your address bar. It looks like linkedin.com/in/jane-cooper.

  2. 2

    Paste it into the contact finder

    Drop the LinkedIn URL into the box above and click Get contact info.

  3. 3

    Get phone and email together

    We look up the profile in our 690M+ record database and return the work email, direct dial and office number we hold.

  4. 4

    Reach out on the right channel

    Call the direct dial, or send a cold email to the verified work address.

How to get contact info from LinkedIn without a connection

LinkedIn only shows the Contact info section to your 1st-degree connections, and most people leave it empty anyway. Waiting for a connection request to be accepted can take weeks.

A LinkedIn profile lookup skips that wait. We match the public profile URL to business records, so you get the phone number and email whether or not you are connected.

  • No connection request needed
  • No Sales Navigator seat needed
  • No Chrome extension reading your session
  • Works on any public profile URL

Where the contact info comes from

We do not scrape LinkedIn or log in with your account. The profile URL is used as a key to match the person in our B2B database, which is built from public professional sources and business records and refreshed regularly.

Results are for business outreach. Personal email addresses are hidden, numbers on do-not-call lists are flagged, and anyone can ask us to remove their data.
